Lock and unlock your door from Homey, keep an eye on the battery, and see who opened it and how, using your existing TTLock account. Build flows around your door: turn on a light when someone unlocks it with a fingerprint, get a notification on a tamper alert, or lock up when everyone has left. The app also reports gateway signal strength, door sensor state, Auto Lock, Lock Sound and Passage Mode.

You need a TTLock smart lock connected to a TTLock Gateway, and your own TTLock OAuth application from the TTLock Open Platform, as TTLock requires every app to be registered individually. Enter its Client ID and Secret in the app's settings before adding your lock. Real-time updates and the alert cards need the TTLock webhook, which the app's settings page helps you set up. Without it, the app checks your lock every 30 minutes.
